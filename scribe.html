Meet The Scribe.

Think you know your favourite stories? The Scribe brings quote-guessing challenges to your Discord server, with hints, daily quotes, and scores to keep track of who knows their lines.

Invite The ScribeTry it in our Discord
What it can do

Put your quote knowledge to the test.

A mix of quick challenges and friendly competition for your server.

🎯

Guess the source

Read a quote and work out which series, game, film, show, or book it comes from.

💡

Hints when you need them

Use hints to give yourself a nudge when a quote feels familiar but the answer will not come to mind.

📅

Daily quotes

Give your community a regular quote challenge to come back to.

🏆

Persistent leaderboards

Keep track of scores over time and see who is leading the quote challenge.

📚

Multiple categories

Quotes span anime and manga, video games, films and TV, and books and fantasy.

✍️

Community submissions

Server members can submit quotes, helping the collection grow with the things your community loves.

Getting started

Ready in a few steps.

1. Add the bot

Use the invite button above and choose the Discord server where you have permission to add bots.

2. Start a challenge

Use /guess in your server to take part in a quote challenge.

3. Ask for a hint

Need a clue? Use /hint to get a little help with the quote.
