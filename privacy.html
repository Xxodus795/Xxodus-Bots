Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This draft explains the intended privacy approach for The Scribe only. It must be checked against the actual bot code, database, hosting provider, logs, backups, and enabled features before publication. This policy does not currently cover other Xxodus Bots projects.

Information The Scribe may process

Depending on the features you use and the server’s configuration, The Scribe may process Discord user IDs to associate scores with players; server and channel IDs to operate server-specific features or scheduled posts; scores and related game progress; and quote text or other content submitted through bot commands. Discord may also provide interaction or technical information needed to deliver commands. Confirm which of these the deployed version actually stores, and remove any item that does not apply.

How information is used

Information is used only as needed to operate The Scribe’s features, such as quote guessing, hints, scores and leaderboards, user submissions, scheduled posts, troubleshooting, security, and service maintenance.

Storage, providers, and sharing

Information may be stored in the database configured for The Scribe and processed by the service or hosting providers needed to run it. Before treating this policy as final, identify the actual hosting provider, where the database and backups are kept, whether logs are retained, and any other services that receive data. The operator should not sell personal data. Information may need to be disclosed where required by law or to address security, abuse, or service-protection issues.

Retention and deletion requests

Information should be kept only for as long as needed for the purposes above. The actual retention periods for scores, submissions, logs, backups, and server configuration must be confirmed and stated once established. You can ask a question or request deletion by contacting us through the Xxodus Bots Discord support server. We may need your Discord user ID or relevant server details to locate the data. Some copies may remain in backups for a limited period, or information may need to be retained where the law requires it.

Your choices and third-party services

You can choose not to use The Scribe or its optional submission features. Discord’s own privacy policy and terms also apply to your use of Discord. The operator does not control Discord’s independent data practices.

Children, security, and policy changes

Do not submit sensitive personal information through the bot. Reasonable steps should be taken to protect stored information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated as The Scribe changes; the current version will be published at https://xxodus795.github.io/Xxodus-Bots/.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Xxodus Bots through the Xxodus Bots Discord support server. A Discord invite is a contact route, not a guarantee that messages in the server are private; do not post sensitive information in public channels.

Draft note: Verify this policy against the actual code, database, hosting provider, logs, backups, and retention periods before treating it as final. This is not legal advice.
